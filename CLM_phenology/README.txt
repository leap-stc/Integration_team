Objective: Develop ML-parameterization of lichen for the community land model. This is a new parameterization to represent lichen and the trapping of particulate matter in the air like dust, while also absorbing smaller pollutants like sulfur, mercury, and nitrogen.

Training data: linnia's lichen library

Timeline:

    January 2025: Project scoping with PI, team, & integration team member
    Spring 2025: design experiment, collect training data, explore architectures.
    Summer 2025: Trained offline ML module complete.
    Fall 2025: begin implementation in CLM and online testing.

Outcome: Point scale simulations of carbon and nitrogen cycling at NEON experimental sites with and without new lichen parameterization. Demonstration of model functional improvement.
